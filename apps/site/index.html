What it does

Built for showing the work, not editing it.

  1. 01

    Reads your RAW files

    NEF, CR2, CR3 and ARW, plus JPEG, TIFF and PNG. Fast previews, and the original files are never touched.

  2. 02

    Shows the camera data

    Lens, focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO — and where the camera actually focused, drawn on the frame.

  3. 03

    Caption and review

    Write the story behind each photograph. Reviewers can leave comments frame by frame.

  4. 04

    Present fullscreen

    A clean metadata overlay you control, keyboard and touch navigation, and nothing else on screen.

  5. 05

    Share one file

    Export a project as a single .fovea file. It opens on a Mac, a Windows PC, or a phone's browser.

  6. 06

    Stays on your device

    No accounts, no uploads, no analytics, no AI. Works offline once it's loaded.
